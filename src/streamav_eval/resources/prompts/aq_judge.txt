# Task

Evaluate the audio quality of the provided generated audio-video clip.

Assess two aspects: audio naturalness and audio artifacts.

Assign a score from 1 to 5 to each aspect.

# Scoring Criteria

Audio Naturalness

1 = The audio sounds highly unnatural, implausible, or strongly synthetic.
2 = The audio remains recognizable but sounds noticeably artificial, with
    poor spatial plausibility or weak acoustic fit to the scene.
3 = The audio is generally plausible, though noticeable synthetic qualities
    or imperfect scene fit remain.
4 = The audio sounds natural, clear, and appropriate to the scene, with only
    minor unnaturalness.
5 = The audio is highly natural, clear, spatially plausible, and convincing
    within the generated scene.


Audio Artifacts

1 = Severe technical artifacts such as clipping, buzzing, dropouts, glitches,
    abrupt silence, or repetitive loops strongly disrupt the audio.
2 = Obvious artifacts occur frequently and noticeably interfere with listening.
3 = Artifacts are noticeable, but the audio remains generally usable.
4 = Only minor or occasional artifacts are present.
5 = The audio is clean and stable, with no obvious technical artifacts.

# Instructions

Score the two aspects independently and provide a brief overall reason.

Do not evaluate visual quality or frame-level synchronization, and score artifacts only for technical audio defects.

Return strict JSON with no additional text.

# Output Format

{
  "audio_naturalness": <1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5>,
  "audio_artifacts": <1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5>,
  "reason": "<short reason>"
}
