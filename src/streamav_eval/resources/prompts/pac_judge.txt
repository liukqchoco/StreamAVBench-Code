# Task

Evaluate the audio transition stability of the provided 4-second audio clip,
covering 2 seconds before and 2 seconds after a runtime-update boundary.

Current runtime update:
{current_update}

Judge whether the audio remains continuous and technically stable across the
boundary. First identify the audio changes allowed by the update, then penalize
only unintended defects that are separable from those changes.

Inspect for:
- unintended audio dropouts or abrupt silence;
- unintended hard cuts or broken ongoing sound;
- unintended repetition, looping, or stutter;
- spurious clicks, pops, buzzing, or clipping;
- unintended sudden volume jumps or other obvious boundary artifacts.

# Stability Scale

1 = No Stability: A severe audio break or corruption occurs at the boundary,
    making the transition unusable.
2 = Low Stability: Major boundary defects are clearly audible and strongly
    disrupt continuity.
3 = Moderate Stability: The transition remains understandable, but noticeable
    discontinuity, dropout, or artifact is present.
4 = High Stability: The transition is coherent overall, with only minor
    boundary artifacts.
5 = Full Stability: The transition is aurally continuous and stable, with no
    obvious unintended boundary defect.

# Diagnostic Fields

- audio_break: true only if an unintended dropout, abrupt silence, hard cut,
  broken ongoing sound, repetition, loop, or stutter occurs at the boundary.
- audio_artifact: true only if an unintended click, pop, buzz, clipping event,
  sudden volume jump, or comparable technical artifact occurs at the boundary.

The diagnostic fields support failure analysis and do not replace the score.
Return strict JSON with no additional text.

# Output Format

{
  "score": <1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5>,
  "audio_break": <true | false>,
  "audio_artifact": <true | false>,
  "reason": "<short reason>"
}
