# Task

Evaluate the visual transition stability of the provided 4-second video clip,
covering 2 seconds before and 2 seconds after a runtime-update boundary.

Current runtime update:
{current_update}

Judge whether the video remains visually continuous and technically stable
across the boundary. First identify the visual changes allowed by the update,
then penalize only unintended defects that are separable from those changes.

Inspect for:
- black frames, flashes, flickering, freezing, or duplicate-frame stalls;
- abrupt visual discontinuity or generation break;
- abnormal subject deformation;
- broken or discontinuous action;
- unintended object or subject disappearance.

# Stability Scale

1 = No Stability: A severe generation break or corruption occurs at the
    boundary, making the transition unusable.
2 = Low Stability: Major boundary defects are clearly visible and strongly
    disrupt continuity.
3 = Moderate Stability: The transition remains understandable, but noticeable
    discontinuity, deformation, or local instability is present.
4 = High Stability: The transition is coherent overall, with only minor
    boundary artifacts.
5 = Full Stability: The transition is visually continuous and stable, with no
    obvious unintended boundary defect.

# Diagnostic Fields

- generation_break: true only if an unintended abrupt discontinuity,
  black/flash/freeze event, or broken action occurs at the boundary.
- deformation: true only if an unintended structural distortion of a subject
  or object occurs at the boundary.
- object_disappear: true only if an unintended subject or object disappearance
  occurs at the boundary.

The diagnostic fields support failure analysis and do not replace the score.
Return strict JSON with no additional text.

# Output Format

{
  "score": <1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5>,
  "generation_break": <true | false>,
  "deformation": <true | false>,
  "object_disappear": <true | false>,
  "reason": "<short reason>"
}
