# Task

Evaluate the visual quality of the provided generated video.

Assess four aspects: visual fidelity, subject integrity, motion naturalness,
and visual artifacts.

Assign a score from 1 to 5 to each aspect.

# Scoring Criteria

Visual Fidelity

1 = Visual quality is severely degraded by blur, exposure errors,
    compression, corruption, or other major failures.
2 = Visual quality is poor, with frequent degradation that noticeably
    affects viewing.
3 = Visual quality is acceptable but contains noticeable quality defects.
4 = Visual quality is good overall, with only minor imperfections.
5 = Visual content is sharp, clear, properly exposed, and well rendered.


Subject Integrity

1 = Main subjects are severely deformed or difficult to recognize.
2 = Subjects are recognizable but frequently distorted, unstable, or
    structurally broken.
3 = Subjects are mostly recognizable, with noticeable but tolerable
    identity or geometry inconsistencies.
4 = Subjects remain stable and structurally intact, with only minor defects.
5 = Subjects are consistently clear, complete, and visually coherent.


Motion Naturalness

1 = Motion is severely broken, physically incoherent, or impossible to
    meaningfully assess.
2 = Motion is largely stiff, incorrect, or visibly unnatural.
3 = Motion is understandable but contains noticeable jitter, irregularity,
    or unnatural dynamics.
4 = Motion is natural and coherent overall, with only minor local issues.
5 = Motion is smooth, fluent, and physically plausible.


Visual Artifacts

1 = Severe generation artifacts dominate the visual content.
2 = Obvious artifacts occur frequently and substantially disrupt viewing.
3 = Artifacts are noticeable but the video remains generally viewable.
4 = Only minor or occasional artifacts are visible.
5 = No obvious generation artifacts are observed.

# Instructions

Score the four aspects independently and provide a brief overall reason.

Return strict JSON with no additional text.

# Output Format

{
  "visual_fidelity": <1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5>,
  "subject_integrity": <1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5>,
  "motion_naturalness": <1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5>,
  "visual_artifacts": <1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5>,
  "reason": "<short reason>"
}
